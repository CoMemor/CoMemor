CoMemor Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you join our waitlist, visit our website, or use our developer tools.

Summary in Plain English: We use your contact information solely to communicate with you about the CoMemor Team Cloud waitlist and product updates. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. The open-source CoMemor MCP server is 100% local-first and transmits zero code, logs, or telemetry to our servers.

1. Two Distinct Products: Local MCP vs. Cloud Waitlist

It is important to understand the boundary between CoMemor's open-source tool and our cloud waitlist:

  • The Open-Source MCP Server (npx comemor): Operates entirely on your local workstation or build machine via stdio. All memory files (.agent-memory/records.jsonl) and SQLite indexes remain local. It makes zero outbound cloud connections, collects zero telemetry, and never sends your source code, build failures, or secrets anywhere.
  • The CoMemor Website & Waitlist (comemor.tech): When you register for our upcoming Team Cloud early access program, we collect the contact details you voluntarily provide as described below.

2. Information We Collect on the Waitlist

When you register for the CoMemor Team Cloud waitlist or fill out our sign-up forms, we may collect:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, and optional company or organization name.
  • Developer Context: Your role, team size, and the AI coding environments you use (e.g., Cursor, Claude Desktop, VS Code, Windsurf, or Google Antigravity).
  • Feedback & Inquiries: Any optional notes, use cases, or feature requests you choose to share in registration form fields.
  • Basic Web Infrastructure Logs: Non-personally identifiable technical details (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and request timestamp) captured automatically by our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages) for DDoS prevention and service stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information based on your voluntary consent to:

  • Send you notifications when your position on the CoMemor Team Cloud waitlist becomes active.
  • Deliver onboarding documentation, access links, and technical updates related to your beta participation.
  • Share product announcements, security advisories, and critical updates regarding CoMemor.
  • Analyze aggregate, anonymized feedback to prioritize which host integrations and features to build next.

We will never sell, trade, rent, or distribute your personal information to third-party marketers, data brokers, or advertisers.

4. Communications & Opt-Out

You have full control over your communications:

  • Every announcement or waitlist update email will include an immediate, one-click Unsubscribe link.
  • You can also email us directly at any time at prasannas1104@gmail.com with the subject "Unsubscribe" or "Delete My Data" to have your details permanently removed from our waitlist records.

5. Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to securely host our website and manage waitlist communications:

  • Cloudflare & GitHub Pages: Provide CDN edge routing, SSL/TLS certificate termination, and static asset hosting under enterprise-grade security protocols.
  • Waitlist & Form Processors: We use industry-standard form services (such as Google Forms or Typeform) and email delivery services with end-to-end encryption to store registrant records securely.

6. Security Measures & Potential Risks

We employ appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information, including enforced HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption across all website endpoints and restricted access limited only to authorized maintainers.

Important Risk Disclosures for Registrants:

  • Early-Stage Program: CoMemor Team Cloud is currently in pre-release development. While we take security seriously, no internet data transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% invulnerable.
  • No Sensitive Code or Secrets: Please do not submit passwords, private API keys, production tokens, or confidential proprietary code inside free-text waitlist fields. Waitlist forms are intended solely for contact information and high-level developer context.

7. Data Retention & Your Legal Rights

We retain your contact details only for as long as necessary to fulfill waitlist management and early access onboarding, or until you request deletion. Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate contact information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that your contact details be permanently deleted from our waitlist and marketing lists.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent to receive future communications at any time.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact:

  • Email: prasannas1104@gmail.com
  • GitHub Repository: https://github.com/PrasannaSaiS/CoMemor
  • Official Website: https://comemor.tech
